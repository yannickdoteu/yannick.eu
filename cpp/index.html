C++ modules: upgrading from C to C++ in weeks

One of the core philosophies of École 42 is that by focussing on the core principles of programming, in an environment where things are 'harder to do'. That's why the first 9 projects are to be written in C. By having this approach, the idea is that you are able to quickly pick up any other language or framework, because they are all based on, or translatable to the same fundamental principles.

To demonstrate this during the Common Core, you get a kind-of second 'mini piscine' about Ojbect Oriented Programming that is C++.
